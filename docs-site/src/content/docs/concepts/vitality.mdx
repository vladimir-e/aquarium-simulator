## The principle

Designing a mechanic is choosing rates of benefit and harm. Every organism with
a condition runs the same model: condition is the buffer between dying and
thriving, normal at 100. Benefit rates raise it and damage rates lower it, so
nothing dies or heals instantly.

A threshold that switches a mechanism on or off is a defect; a rate that starts
at a tolerance edge is not ([Rates into stocks](/concepts/rates-into-stocks/#where-switches-still-live)).

## One hour

Each hour an organism lists what is doing it good and what is harming it, both
in condition points per hour, and one shared step settles them.

```
condition += Σbenefits − Σstressors
above 100:  the excess banks, up to surplusCap, and condition sits at 100
below 100:  the bank heals min(100 − condition, healingRate × bank)
```

| Term | Is |
|---|---|
| Benefits | Income from favourable conditions. Hardiness never scales them |
| Stressors | Damage, hardened by each organism: `× (1 − hardiness)`, or by moving where harm starts |
| Bank | Condition points banked at full condition, capped at `surplusCap` |
| Healing rate | The most of its bank an organism heals from in an hour, as a share |

Income heals first, and only what a full condition leaves over banks. Damage
never touches the bank; it lowers condition, and the bank heals it back. Healing
is first order: a bank pays out at most a share of itself each hour. Under
damage heavier than that share it drains on an exponential with a time constant
of `1 / healingRate` hours; under lighter damage it pays out only what the
damage took, and drains in a straight line.

## Sickness and immunity

The bank feeding condition is the organism's immunity. A full bank heals up to
`healingRate × surplusCap` an hour, which is what lets a banked organism ride
out a bad spell at full condition while an unbanked one loses condition from
the first hour.

An organism is sick while its damage outruns its healing, so its condition
falls. Sickness is not a separate mechanic: the damage rate *is* the sickness,
and it ends when the rates turn round.

| Organism | Healing rate | Shipped |
|---|---|---|
| Fish | `healingDrawRate × adultMass^−¼`, the way mass-specific metabolism scales | 6 %/h for a neon, 2.5 %/h for an angelfish |
| Plant | `healingDrawRate × growthRate` — a plant repairs at the pace it grows | 9 %/h for monte carlo, 1.5 %/h for anubias |

## What the bank buys

| Organism | Low condition | The bank buys |
|---|---|---|
| Fish | Dies at 0 | A brood: a full bank buys a spawn, and the spawn empties it — see [Breeding](/subsystems/livestock/#breeding) |
| Plant | Sheds tissue, with the square of the deficit; dies at 0 | Its growth and its offshoots: growth draws on the bank through a taper that closes as the unit fills, and a full bank buys a new unit of the family — see [Offshoots](/subsystems/plants/#offshoots). New tissue is drawn from the water, and the bank pays only for what arrived |

## Algae runs its own bank

Algae keeps no condition. A bloom is a population, not an individual, so its net
rate folds straight into a bank of its own: damage drains the bank before it
touches mass, and the bank buys mass. See [Algae](/subsystems/algae/).

| Mechanic | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The balance | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | One step for fish and plants. Each organism hardens its own charges, so a channel can move its edge instead of scaling its charge |
| Banking at full condition | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Income heals first and banks only at 100; damage never reaches the bank |
| First-order healing | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | The bank heals at a species share, with no floor and no switch — the same law growth draws on |
| Sickness | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | A reading of the rates, not a state: condition falling is the whole of it |
| Tolerance-band benefits | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Graded to zero at each edge, and the matching damage starts from zero at or past it, so net never jumps as a reading leaves its band |
| Brood and offshoot | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Each is an event a full bank buys: the bank is its price and refilling it is the cooldown, with no timers |
| Algae's bank | <Badge text="scaffolding" variant="caution" /> | It banks and grows only while the lamp is on — a switch on light, not a rate |
