## The principle

Designing a mechanic is choosing rates of benefit and harm. Every organism with
a condition runs the same model: condition is the buffer between dying and
thriving, normal at 100. Benefit rates raise it and damage rates lower it, so
nothing dies or heals instantly.

A threshold that switches a mechanism on or off is a defect; a rate that starts
at a tolerance edge is not ([Rates into stocks](/concepts/rates-into-stocks/#switches-and-what-only-looks-like-one)).

## One hour

Each hour an organism lists what is doing it good and what is harming it, both
in condition points per hour, and one shared step settles them.

```
condition += Σbenefits − Σstressors
above 100:  the excess banks, up to surplusCap, and condition sits at 100
below 100:  the bank heals min(100 − condition, (1 − e^−healingRate) × bank)
```

| Term | Is |
|---|---|
| Benefits | Income from favourable conditions, earned on what the organism takes in — a plant's light and nutrients, a fish's digestion. Hardiness never scales them |
| Stressors | Damage, hardened by each organism: `× (1 − hardiness)`, or by moving where harm starts |
| Bank | Condition points banked at full condition, capped at `surplusCap` |
| Healing rate | The first-order rate its bank drains at while it heals, per hour |

Income heals first, and only what a full condition leaves over banks. Damage
never touches the bank; it lowers condition, and the bank heals it back. Healing
is first order, integrated over the hour: a bank pays out at most
`1 − e^−healingRate` of itself, so under damage heavier than that it drains on an
exponential with a time constant of `1 / healingRate` hours, however fast the
rate. Under lighter damage it pays out only what the damage took, and drains in
a straight line.

## Sickness and immunity

The bank feeding condition is the organism's immunity. A full bank heals up to
`(1 − e^−healingRate) × surplusCap` in an hour, which is what lets a banked
organism ride out a bad spell at full condition while an unbanked one loses
condition from the first hour.

An organism is sick while its damage outruns its healing, so its condition
falls. Sickness is not a separate mechanic: the damage rate *is* the sickness,
and it ends when the rates turn round.

| Organism | Healing rate | Shipped |
|---|---|---|
| Fish | `healingDrawRate × adultMass^−¼`, the way mass-specific metabolism scales | 0.06 /h for a neon, 0.025 /h for an angelfish |
| Plant | `healingDrawRate × growthRate` — a plant repairs at the rate it grows | 0.09 /h for monte carlo, 0.015 /h for anubias |
| Algae | The plants' law, at its kind's growth rate | 3 /h for green water — 95 % of its bank in an hour — and 1 /h for film, 63 % |

## What the bank buys

| Organism | Low condition | The bank buys |
|---|---|---|
| Fish | Dies at 0 | A brood: a full bank buys a spawn, and the spawn empties it — see [Breeding](/subsystems/livestock/#breeding) |
| Plant | Sheds tissue, with the square of the deficit; dies at 0 | Its growth and its offshoots: growth draws on the bank through a taper that closes as the unit fills, and a full bank buys a new unit of the family — see [Offshoots](/subsystems/plants/#offshoots) |
| Algae | Sheds mass as a plant sheds tissue; dies back at 0 | Mass, in proportion to the mass standing, on the logistic its habitat sets — see [Algae](/subsystems/algae/#the-bank-buys-mass). Its spores are its offshoots: they land at condition 100 on an empty bank whatever the bloom is doing |

## Status

| Mechanic | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The balance | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | One step for fish, plants and the blooms. Each organism hardens its own charges, so a channel can move its edge instead of scaling its charge |
| Banking at full condition | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Income heals first and banks only at 100; damage never reaches the bank |
| First-order healing | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | The bank heals at a species rate, integrated over the hour, with no floor, switch or clamp |
| Sickness | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | A reading of the rates, not a state: condition falling is the whole of it |
| Tolerance-band benefits | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Graded to zero at each edge, and the matching damage starts from zero at or past it, so net never jumps as a reading leaves its band |
| Brood and offshoot | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Each is an event a full bank buys: the bank is its price and refilling it is the cooldown, with no timers |
