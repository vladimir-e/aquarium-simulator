## The principle

Designing a mechanic is choosing rates of benefit and harm. Every organism with
a condition runs the same model: condition is the buffer between dying and
thriving, normal at 100. Benefit rates raise it and damage rates lower it, so
nothing dies or heals instantly.

A threshold that switches a mechanism on or off is a defect; a rate that starts
at a tolerance edge is not ([Rates into stocks](/concepts/rates-into-stocks/#switches-and-what-only-looks-like-one)).

## One hour

Each hour an organism lists what is doing it good and what is harming it, both
in condition points per hour, and one shared step settles them, with the repair
its energy budget paid for — fish, plants and the blooms alike.

| Term | Is |
|---|---|
| Benefits | Income from favourable conditions, earned on how fed its reserve is — a plant's and a bloom's also on how full its stores run. Hardiness never scales them |
| Stressors | Damage, hardened by each organism: `× (1 − hardiness)`, or by moving where harm starts |
| Starvation | One stressor among them: the share of the hour's upkeep its budget left unpaid |
| Repair | A first-order share of the deficit at `healingRate` and the organism's pace, priced at `healingCost` hours of its own upkeep a point and paid from its reserve inside the budget's hour. An empty reserve repairs nothing |

Nothing banks: condition past 100 is lost. The reserve is the buffer — it pays
maintenance first and repair after, so a fed organism repairs as it is harmed
and a starving one cannot.

## Sickness

An organism is sick while its damage outruns its income and repair, so its
condition falls. Sickness is not a separate mechanic: the damage rate *is* the
sickness, and it ends when the rates turn round.

| Organism | Repair | Shipped |
|---|---|---|
| Fish | At its Arrhenius pace | 0.005 /h at 20 °C, half an hour of upkeep a point |
| Plant · bloom | At the flora's Q10 pace, on the plants' constants | 0.05 /h at 25 °C, an hour of upkeep a point |

## What the budget buys

| Organism | Low condition | Its budget buys |
|---|---|---|
| Fish | Dies at 0 | Growth and broods — see [The budget](/subsystems/livestock/#the-budget) |
| Plant | Sheds tissue, with the square of the deficit; dies at 0 | Growth on its least-filled store, and the plantlet its offshoot buffer builds — see [The budget](/subsystems/plants/#the-budget) |
| Algae | Sheds as a plant does; dies back at 0 | Growth alone, its whole release to the soma: its growth is its reproduction — see [Algae](/subsystems/algae/#the-budget) |

## Status

| Mechanic | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The balance | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | One step for every organism. Each hardens its own charges, so a channel can move its edge instead of scaling its charge |
| Nothing banks | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Condition past 100 is lost; the reserve is the only buffer, and maintenance comes before repair |
| Repair from reserve | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | First order on the deficit at the organism's pace, priced in its own upkeep, paid inside its budget's hour — no floor, switch or clamp |
| Sickness | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | A reading of the rates, not a state: condition falling is the whole of it |
| Tolerance-band benefits | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Graded to zero at each edge, and the matching damage starts from zero at or past it, so net never jumps as a reading leaves its band |
| Offspring | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | A buffer the reserve's other share fills — a fish's brood, a plant's plantlet — released once it holds one: filling it is the price and the cooldown, with no timers |
