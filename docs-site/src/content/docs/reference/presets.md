A preset is a tank configuration paired with an optional seed, so loading one
opens on a tank that is already somewhere in its life rather than on hour zero
of an empty box. Five ship with the engine, and `planted` is the default.

## The five tanks

| Preset | The tank it represents | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| Bare Tank | 40 L with nothing in it: no heater, no filter, no light, no substrate, bare glass | The uncycled floor. No colony, no bed to leach from, and no surface beyond the glass — so the tank drifts to room temperature and stays chemically inert until something is added to it |
| Betta Cube | 20 L nano, sponge filter, gravel, mesh lid, a low fixture on an 8 h day, heated to 26 °C in a 22 °C room off 18 °C tap | A small heated tank against a cold room. Low volume is the point: the same milligram reads several times higher here, and a water change swings the temperature the heater then has to pay back |
| Planted Tank | 40 L, canister, aqua soil, no lid, no heater, a bright fixture on a 12 h day, CO₂ injected on a 10 h schedule, auto top-off on | The high-tech planted case, and an unheated one. CO₂ on a schedule swings pH across the day; an open top evaporates and the ATO answers it |
| Balanced Community | 150 L, canister, aqua soil, heated to 27 °C, moderate light on a 10 h day, seven hardscape items | A large, well-equipped tank at rest. Volume and biofilter surface both scale up, so the same husbandry mistake reads far smaller here than in the cube |
| Big Angelfish Tank | 300 L, canister, sand, heated to 26 °C on 200 W, moderate light on a 12 h day | Scale, on a lean bed. Sand carries a third of aqua soil's colony surface and a fraction of its organic reserve, and the 200 W heater is the same element the 150 L community runs |

## What a preset carries

| Half | Contents |
|---|---|
| Configuration | Capacity, room and tap water conditions, and every device the tank is built with — heater, filter, powerhead, lid, substrate, hardscape, light, CO₂, ATO, doser |
| Seed | The state it opens at. Four of the five seed `'cycled'`; the bare tank seeds nothing |

No shipped preset stocks fish or plants. The tanks are hardware and water; the
livestock is the consumer's to add.

## What `'cycled'` means

`'cycled'` is a claim about a tank that has been running a month, not only about
its biofilter. It resolves against the tank at the moment the seed is applied,
so a preset resized or rescaped at the door still gets a colony, a bed and a
nitrate reading that fit it.

| Stock | Seeded to |
|---|---|
| AOB and NOB | The colony a tank of that capacity rests at once cycled, per litre of water |
| Substrate organic reserve | The share of a fresh bed still unleached on day 30 |
| Nitrate | What a month of leaching leaves behind, discounted for the water changes a keeper would have done |

A tank seeded this way is handed the outcome, not a shortcut through the
mechanism: the tick loop takes it from there, and nothing catches up afterward.
