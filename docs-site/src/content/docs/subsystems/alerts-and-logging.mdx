## What it models

Seven threshold checks run at the end of every tick, after every effect has
landed. Each holds a flag: it writes one line when the reading crosses into
trouble, stays quiet while the condition persists, and clears silently when the
reading comes back. Alongside them the log collects everything the tank did that
a reader would want to see afterwards.

## Status

| Mechanic | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The latch | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | One line per crossing, a flag that lives in state, and no repetition while the condition holds. It does exactly what it says. |
| Log entry shape | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Free text for humans, a typed `event` discriminator for code, and a `count` wherever an entry accounts for more than one organism. |
| Water-quality thresholds | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Free NH₃, nitrite, nitrate and oxygen alert at their tolerance edge, and hardiness moves each fish's own harm edge out from it. Ammonia reads the free fraction, so the total it takes moves with pH and temperature. |
| Algae · water-level thresholds | <Badge text="scaffolding" variant="caution" /> | These lag harm rather than lead it: algae alerts at 80 but shades plants from 30, and water level alerts at 20 % while fish suffer from 50 %. |
| CO₂ threshold | <Badge text="scaffolding" variant="caution" /> | The alert with nothing behind it — no organism in the engine reads dissolved CO₂ as a stressor, so it warns about a harm that is never inflicted. |
| Behaviour on a drained tank | <Badge text="scaffolding" variant="caution" /> | Concentrations read 0 when there is no water and the water-level check requires `water > 0`, so the alert layer goes silent exactly when the tank is empty. Fish health reads the same zeroes, so a drained tank charges no toxin and no flow — its water-level stressor carries it. |
| Clearing | <Badge text="missing" variant="danger" /> | A condition resolving writes nothing. The log records that a tank got into trouble and never that it got out. |
| Retention | <Badge text="missing" variant="danger" /> | The array is appended to and never trimmed, so a long-running tank grows its log without bound. |
| Log persistence | <Badge text="missing" variant="danger" /> | Logs are not saved. A reload starts a fresh history while the latch flags survive it, so an alert already tripped never re-fires its line for the same episode. |

## How it works

### Two channels

| Channel | Lives in | Shape | Survives a save |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alert flags | `state.alertState` | one boolean per condition | yes |
| Log | `state.logs` | an append-only array of entries | no |

The flags are what make an alert stateful. The check compares the reading
against its threshold, compares that against the flag, and emits a line only on
the transition into trouble.

### The alerts

| Alert | Fires when | Where damage actually starts |
|---|---|---|
| Water level critical | below 20 % of capacity, and only while some water remains | fish take water-level damage below 50 % |
| High ammonia | free NH₃ above 0.02 ppm | past the line for every fish, further out the hardier it is |
| High nitrite | above 0.5 ppm | the same |
| High nitrate | above 80 ppm | the same |
| Low oxygen | below 4 mg/L | the same, below the line |
| High CO₂ | above 30 mg/L | nowhere — nothing reads it |
| High algae | mass at or above 80 | plants are shaded from mass 30 |

The four water-quality alerts fire before any fish takes damage. Water level
and algae wait until the reading is worse than that point, and CO₂ has no harm
behind it at all. Ammonia reads the unionized fraction of the total, so the same
test-kit reading crosses it sooner the higher the pH and the warmer the water:

| pH at 25 °C | Total ammonia at the line |
|---|---|
| 6.5 | ≈ 11 ppm |
| 7.0 | ≈ 3.6 ppm |
| 7.5 | ≈ 1.1 ppm |
| 8.0 | ≈ 0.37 ppm |

Every surface bands the total-ammonia reading at that moving line.

### What gets logged

The log is an event stream, not a time series. A quiet tank running for a
thousand ticks writes nothing at all: no readings are sampled, no summary is
emitted per hour, and nothing is written unless something happened.

| Source | Written when |
|---|---|
| `simulation` | The tank is built; a fish or plant dies; a spawn, a laying, a hatch or an offshoot |
| `user` | Any applied action — a refused one logs nothing |
| `scrub` | The scrub action, filed under its own channel |
| `evaporation` · `nitrogen-cycle` · `gas-exchange` · `algae` | The alert lines, each under the system whose reading tripped |

Severity has two values. Every alert line and every death is a `warning`;
everything else is `info`.

### The entry

| Field | Holds |
|---|---|
| `tick` | The hour it happened, which is what makes the log reconstructable |
| `source` | The system that emitted it |
| `severity` | `info` or `warning` |
| `message` | Free text, for humans only |
| `event` | Present only on the lifecycle moments a caller reacts to |
| `count` | Present where one entry accounts for many organisms |

Seven typed events exist: `fish-spawned`, `eggs-laid`, `eggs-hatched`,
`fish-died`, `plant-died`, `plant-propagated` and `fry-sold`. Three of them
carry a count — a live birth, a hatch and a fry sale — because the number is the
point. Deaths and offshoots are one entry per organism, so they need none.

The discriminator is the contract. A caller that parses the free text is
reading a field that exists to be rewritten.

### The log as the tank's history

Every line carries the tick it happened at, so the array is a reconstructable
timeline of everything the tank did. It is also the only such record: the
state is a snapshot of one hour, carrying no more past than the last day's
light.

That makes two absences load-bearing. The array is never trimmed, so it grows
for as long as the tank runs. And it is not part of the persisted state, so
saving and reloading gives a tank with its full biology intact and no memory of
how it got there.

## Key tunables

None of these is a `TunableConfig` leaf. They are module constants with no
declared range, so they cannot be moved through the config layer the way a
severity or a rate can. The four water-quality lines are the fish tolerance
edges, defined with [livestock](/subsystems/livestock/).

| Constant | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| `WATER_LEVEL_CRITICAL_THRESHOLD` | Share of capacity below which the level is critical | `0.2` |
| `FREE_AMMONIA_EDGE` | Free (unionized) NH₃ at which the alert fires | `0.02` ppm |
| `NITRITE_EDGE` | Nitrite at which the alert fires | `0.5` ppm |
| `NITRATE_EDGE` | Nitrate at which the alert fires | `80` ppm |
| `OXYGEN_EDGE` | Dissolved oxygen below which the alert fires | `4` mg/L |
| `HIGH_CO2_THRESHOLD` | Dissolved CO₂ above which the alert fires | `30` mg/L |
| `HIGH_ALGAE_THRESHOLD` | Algae mass at which the bloom is called | `80` of 100 |
