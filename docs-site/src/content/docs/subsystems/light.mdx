## What it models

A fixture is rated at the water surface, which is the figure a manufacturer
publishes. What the tank runs on is what reaches the substrate, so the rating is
gated by the photoperiod and then carried down the water column by Beer–Lambert.
The result is a single tank-wide scalar, and everything photic in the engine
reads it: photosynthesis, four plant benefit channels, the plant
nutrient-deficiency and light-high stressors, and one algae benefit. The last 24
hours of it are kept, and the plants starve on their integral.

| Mechanic | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|

| Saturation curve | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | `tanh(PAR / Ik)`, the Jassby–Platt response. Near-linear below `Ik`, flat above it — a brighter fixture stops buying anything. |
| Light multiplies the benefit budget | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | Every benefit a plant earns is realised *through* photosynthesis, so light scales all four channels rather than standing beside them. Good water is worth nothing at midnight. |
| `Ik` derived from the species band | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | A fixed multiple of the band's lower bound, so one number carries both the saturation curve and the daily light edge and they cannot drift apart. |
| The day's light | <Badge text="settled" variant="success" /> | A 24-slot history, one slot rewritten each tick. Plants starve on its integral — the day, not the hour. |
| Photoperiod shape | <Badge text="scaffolding" variant="caution" /> | A rectangular window: rated PAR or zero, with no ramp at either edge. |
| What rides on that edge | <Badge text="scaffolding" variant="caution" /> | The algae's two `light > 0` gates, on banking and on growth. The bloom's bank switches on and off with the lamp rather than following a rate. |

## How it works

### The chain

```
depth      = ∛(capacity / 2) × 10                cm
surfacePar = schedule active ? fixture.par : 0   µmol/m²/s
light      = surfacePar × exp(−waterAttenuationPerCm × depth)
```

Clear freshwater loses roughly 1 % of its PAR per centimetre in the 400–700 nm
band, so the loss is modest per cm and considerable over a tank.

| Capacity | Depth | PAR surviving | What a `50` fixture lands |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 L | 21.5 cm | 81 % | 40 |
| 40 L | 27.1 cm | 76 % | 38 |
| 100 L | 36.8 cm | 69 % | 35 |
| 150 L | 42.2 cm | 66 % | 33 |
| 300 L | 53.1 cm | 59 % | 29 |

Fixtures come in four ratings — `25`, `50`, `90`, `150` — the hobby's low,
medium, high and very high tiers. On a 150 L tank they land 16 / 33 / 59 / 98 PAR
at the substrate, which is where those tiers actually sit once the water has
taken its share.

### Saturation

A species answers light along `tanh(PAR / Ik)`, where `Ik` is its saturating
irradiance — derived, not declared, at twice the lower bound of its tolerable
band. At the bottom of its band a plant runs at 46 % of its maximum, at `Ik`
76 %, and at twice `Ik` 96 %.

| Species | Band | `Ik` |
|---|---|---|
| `anubias` | 8–70 PAR | `16` |
| `java_fern` | 10–90 PAR | `20` |
| `amazon_sword` | 20–120 PAR | `40` |
| `dwarf_hairgrass` | 25–200 PAR | `50` |
| `monte_carlo` | 30–200 PAR | `60` |

### Light as the multiplier

The four benefit channels — CO₂, temperature, pH, nutrients — each peak at
`0.125` %/h and are each multiplied by the saturation term, so the budget is
`0.5` %/h × `tanh(PAR / Ik)` and light takes the whole of it down together.

### The day's light

```
lightByHour[tick % 24] = light                       // each tick, as the hour settles
dli  = Σ lightByHour × 3600 / 10⁶                    mol/m²/d
edge = tolerableLight[0] × 8 × 3600 / 10⁶            mol/m²/d, per species
```

The tank keeps the last 24 hours of substrate PAR, one slot per hour of the
day, and the daily light integral is their sum in the hobby's unit. Under a
steady schedule it holds constant through day and night. When the fixture dies
or the schedule shortens, it falls one lit hour at a time as those hours come
round, and reaches zero a day after the last lit hour.

A species starves under the low end of its PAR band held for the 8-hour day care
sheets assume — 0.86 mol/m²/d for monte carlo, 0.23 for anubias. A new tank
opens with the history of the schedule it was built on, as though it had run it
all along.

### Photoperiod

`hourOfDay = tick % 24`, and the schedule is a start hour plus a duration that
wraps midnight cleanly. The default fixture runs 08:00 for ten hours. There is
no dawn and no dusk: the tick before the window the substrate reads 0, the tick
inside it reads the full attenuated rating.

### What reads it

| Consumer | How |
|---|---|
| Photosynthesis | Per plant, `tanh(PAR / Ik)` scales the potential rate that drives O₂, carbon and nutrient uptake |
| Plant benefits | All four channels multiplied by the same term |
| Plant nutrient deficiency | Scaled by the same term |
| Plant starvation | The daily light integral, against the species' edge |
| Plant light-high stressor | PAR over the species band, while the lamp is on |
| Algae | A capped benefit ramp above `70` PAR, and a `light > 0` gate on its banking and growth |

### Stocks and rates

Light is not a stock. It is recomputed from scratch every tick before anything
reads it, and has no source and no drain — only a value. Its history is a record
of those values, rewritten one hour at a time.

## Key tunables

| Constant | Meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| `waterAttenuationPerCm` | Beer–Lambert coefficient of the water column | `0.010` /cm |
| `saturationIrradianceFactor` | Multiple of a species' band-low that gives its `Ik` | `2.0` × |
| `lightStarvationSeverity` | Plant damage in a day without light, at 25 °C | `0.3` %/hr |
| `lightExcessiveSeverity` | Plant damage per PAR unit above the species band | `0.015` %/PAR/hr |
| `lightExcessThreshold` | Substrate PAR above which algae starts earning from light | `70` PAR |
| `MAX_LIGHT_PAR` | Ceiling on a fixture rating — full noon sun at the surface | `2000` µmol/m²/s |

A rating past `MAX_LIGHT_PAR` is a typo rather than a lighting choice, and tank
construction refuses it.
