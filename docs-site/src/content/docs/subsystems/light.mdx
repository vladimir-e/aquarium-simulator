## What it models

A fixture is rated at the water surface, which is the figure a manufacturer
publishes. What the tank runs on is what reaches the substrate, so the rating is
gated by the photoperiod and then carried down the water column by Beer–Lambert,
through the water and the green water suspended in it. Each plant reads that
substrate PAR at its own height, through the water and the canopy above its
leaves and the film coating them, and the last 24 hours of it are kept to read
each plant's day against.

## How it works

### The chain

```
depth      = ∛(capacity / 2) × 10                cm
surfacePar = schedule active ? fixture.par : 0   µmol/m²/s
k          = waterAttenuationPerCm + algaeAttenuationPerCm × green water's coverage / 100
light      = surfacePar × exp(−k × depth)
```

Clear freshwater loses roughly 1 % of its PAR per centimetre in the 400–700 nm
band, so the loss is modest per cm and considerable over a tank. Through clear
water:

| Capacity | Depth | PAR surviving | What a `50` fixture lands |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 L | 21.5 cm | 81 % | 40 |
| 40 L | 27.1 cm | 76 % | 38 |
| 100 L | 36.8 cm | 69 % | 35 |
| 150 L | 42.2 cm | 66 % | 33 |
| 300 L | 53.1 cm | 59 % | 29 |

Fixtures come in four ratings — `25`, `50`, `90`, `150` — the hobby's low,
medium, high and very high tiers. On a 150 L tank they land 16 / 33 / 59 / 98 PAR
at the substrate, which is where those tiers actually sit once the water has
taken its share.

### The blooms

A bloom takes light by Beer–Lambert on its coverage, as it fixes and burns by
it. A kind's habitat decides how the light crosses it.

| Kind | Crossed as | Takes |
|---|---|---|
| Green water | Spread through the column | `algaeAttenuationPerCm` at full coverage, in proportion to it, added to `k`: from the substrate, and from every leaf by the depth above it |
| Film | A coat on every lit surface, leaves included, at its coverage | `coverage × (1 − e^(−algaeCoatOpticalDepth))` of a leaf's light |

At the shipped coefficients a full bloom of green water adds 0.09 per cm to
the water's 0.01, and a full coat of film passes 41 % of a
leaf's light. Through the 42 cm of a 150 L tank green water takes from the
substrate:

| Coverage | Substrate light taken |
|---|---|
| 5 | 17 % |
| 15 | 43 % |
| 30 | 68 % |
| 60 | 90 % |
| 80 | 95 % |

The lamp hangs over the tank, so film on the glass never stands between it and a
plant; only the film on its leaves does.

### Saturation

A species answers light along `tanh(PAR / Ik)`, where `Ik` is its saturating
irradiance — derived, not declared, at `saturationIrradianceFactor` times the
lower bound of its tolerable band. At the shipped factor a plant at the bottom
of its band runs at 46 % of its maximum, at `Ik` 76 %, and at twice `Ik` 96 %. Each species' band and `Ik` are on the
[roster](/subsystems/plants/#the-species-roster).

### Light and income

A plant's three benefit channels are earned on the reserve its photosynthesis
fills, so light reaches its income through the reserve — the budget is under
[Plants](/subsystems/plants/#income-and-harm).

### The canopy

A unit spreads its leaves evenly from the floor to its height, over its own
footprint. Light at a height inside the tank has come through the water above
it and every crown that reaches higher, and each takes its Beer–Lambert share:
the water per cm, the leaves per unit of leaf area index.

```
u_j    = size_j / 100
// crown j's share of the floor
c_j    = footprint_j / (2 × depth²)
// the share of the light crown j takes at height z
τ_j(z) = 1 − exp(−k_L × LAI_j × u_j × max(0, 1 − z / h_j))

// at the mean leaf, z_i = h_i / 2
s_i = f × exp(k × z_i + ½ × k_L × LAI_i × (1 − u_i) − Σ_j≠i c_j × τ_j(z_i))
// at the crown top
χ_i = f × exp(k × h_i − Σ_j≠i c_j × τ_j(h_i))
```

`k` is the water's extinction as it stands, `k_L` is `leafAttenuationPerLai`, and
`f` the share of a leaf's light the film on it passes; a unit's height `h`, leaf
area index and footprint come off its
[growth form](/subsystems/plants/#growth-forms) at its size.

| Term | Reads as |
|---|---|
| `k × z` | The water and green water between the substrate and the leaf, given back: a tall plant's leaves stand nearer the lamp, so a carpet loses the most to green water |
| `f` | The film on its leaves, the same on every plant |
| `½ × k_L × LAI × (1 − u)` | Its own crown, counted against a full unit's. A grown unit alone reads the substrate light plus that water gain; a sparser one is less self-shaded — up to ×2.0 for a sword and ×2.4 for a carpet patch or a clump |
| `Σ c × τ` | Every other crown, placed at random over the floor: each takes at most its floor share, and one no taller than the leaf takes nothing |

A plant reads `light × s_i` at its mean leaf, where it earns, and `light × χ_i`
at its crown top, where it burns — the top leaf has none of its own crown above
it. With `leafAttenuationPerLai` at 0 the leaves take nothing, and a plant reads
the water alone. What reaches the floor is `exp(−Σ c_j × τ_j(0))` of the
substrate light; the rest is the planting's **floor shade**, and the fixture's
rating carried to the floor through both is its **floor light**.

The lamp's rating times the path to a leaf — the water, the canopy, the green
water above it and the film on it, each a share passed — is what the leaf reads
while the lamp is on.

The canopy is built once an hour, off the planting the hour starts with, and
photosynthesis and vitality read the same one.

### The day's light

```
// each tick, as the hour settles
lightByHour[tick % 24] = light
dli  = Σ lightByHour × 3600 / 10⁶                mol/m²/d
edge = tolerableLight[0] × 8 × 3600 / 10⁶        mol/m²/d, per species
```

The tank keeps the last 24 hours of substrate PAR, one slot per hour of the
day, and the daily light integral is their sum in the hobby's unit. Under a
steady schedule it holds constant through day and night. When the fixture dies
or the schedule shortens, it falls one lit hour at a time as those hours come
round, and reaches zero a day after the last lit hour.

A species is rated to live on the low end of its PAR band held for the 8-hour
day care sheets assume — 0.86 mol/m²/d for monte carlo, 0.23 for anubias. Each
plant's day is the integral at its own leaf, `dli × s_i`: today's canopy over
the whole window. Nothing starves on it — a plant starves on its reserve — but
it is the yardstick its light is read against. A new tank opens with the history of the schedule it was built on, read
through the optics it will run on, as though it had run it all along.

### Photoperiod

`hourOfDay = tick % 24`, and the schedule is a start hour plus a duration that
wraps midnight cleanly. The default fixture runs 08:00 for ten hours. There is
no dawn and no dusk: the tick before the window the substrate reads 0, the tick
inside it reads the full attenuated rating.

### What reads it

| Consumer | How |
|---|---|
| Photosynthesis | Per plant, `tanh(PAR / Ik)` at its mean leaf scales the carbon it fixes into its reserve, and the O₂ that comes with it |
| A plant's day | The day's light at its mean leaf, against its rated day |
| Plant light-high stressor | PAR at its crown top over the species band, while the lamp is on |
| Algae | The mean PAR over its habitat, on the plants' curve at its own `Ik`, and the day of it against its own rated day: green water the column's mean — `(e^{kD} − 1) / kD` times the substrate figure, through its own shade — and film the walls at that mean and the floor and hardscape at what the canopy leaves, weighted by area |

### Stocks and rates

Light is not a stock. It is recomputed from scratch every tick before anything
reads it, and has no source and no drain — only a value. Its history is a record
of those values, rewritten one hour at a time. The canopy is no stock either:
it is derived from the planting every hour.

## Key tunables

The optics coefficients are listed in the reference under
[Optics](/reference/tunables/#optics), and the plant constants that read this
light — `saturationIrradianceFactor` and `lightExcessiveSeverity` — under
[Plants](/reference/tunables/#plants).

| Constant | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| `MAX_LIGHT_PAR` | Ceiling on a fixture rating — full noon sun at the surface | `2000` µmol/m²/s |

A rating past `MAX_LIGHT_PAR` is a typo rather than a lighting choice, and tank
construction refuses it.
