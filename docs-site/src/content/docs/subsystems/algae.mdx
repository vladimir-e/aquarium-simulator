## What it models

A bloom is plant mechanics without a position. It has a condition, a bank and
a mass on the [vitality](/concepts/vitality/) model, and its light curve,
income, harms, shedding, bank and tissue recipe are the
[plants'](/subsystems/plants/), on the plants' constants and its own traits. It
has no height or footprint: in their place is a habitat it fills, and its
offshoots are spores that land in it and join it.

The tank holds two kinds, each a bloom of its own on that one model, run
through one code path. They differ by habitat and by traits, and the tank's
conditions pick the winner — there is no split and no coded preference.

| Kind | Habitat | Size of it | Light it reads | Light taken from | Taken out by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Green water | The water column | The tank's litres | The column's mean | Everything below it — itself, the film, each plant by its depth | A water change, by the share changed |
| Film | The lit surfaces that do not grow: the glass walls, the floor and the hardscape | cm² of them | The walls at the column's mean, the floor and hardscape at what the canopy leaves, weighted by area | Every leaf, at its coverage | A scrub, off the glass; a bed swap, off the floor |

Mass is how full the habitat is, 0–100, so a full bloom's tissue scales with
its habitat. Film's habitat moves only with the hardscape, never with the
planting: the leaves wear its coverage but are not habitat, so its tissue is
counted over the fixed surfaces alone.

## How it works

### What feeds it and what harms it

| Channel | Side | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| CO₂ | income | Monod on dissolved CO₂ at its `co2HalfSaturation` — far under any plant's, since algae take bicarbonate too |
| Temperature · pH | income, then harm | The plants' band helpers on its own bands: comfort peaks mid-band, damage starts at the edge |
| Light starvation | harm | The day's light over its habitat against its daily light edge, at respiration's Q10 and its growth rate: a bloom respires fast, so it starves fast |
| Nutrient deficiency | harm | Liebig sufficiency under `sufficiencyEdge`, on the light curve |
| Allelopathy | harm | `allelopathySeverity × Σ(plant rate units × condition/100) / litres of standing water` |

Every harm is hardened by the kind's hardiness. Income runs only while the
lamp is on; the bank carries it through the night.

### The bank buys mass

The rate is the plants': their draw, `1 − e^−growthDrawRate` of the bank, at
their conversion, `growthRate × sizePerSurplus`, is the bloom's specific growth
rate `r` for the hour. Mass follows the logistic exactly over that hour,

```
m₁ = 100·m₀ / (m₀ + (100 − m₀)·e⁻ʳ)
```

and every bank point buys the same e-fold, so the bank pays
`100·ln(m₁ / m₀) / (growthRate × sizePerSurplus)` — never more than the plants'
draw through the taper would. Water that supplies only part of the tissue is
priced the same way: the bank pays for the e-folds that arrived. A small bloom
multiplies, a full habitat buys nothing, and an empty one buys and pays nothing.

### Spores

Spores are the bloom's offshoots. They come at `sporeRate` an hour, through the
taper at the mass the hour's growth reaches at full supply — their tissue is
drawn in the same call as that growth — and land once the bloom's own losses are
taken. They arrive at condition 100 on an empty bank and join the bloom by mass,
so a struggling bloom is lifted by their share of it and its bank is diluted by
the same share. A bloom that died back, or an empty one, is replaced by the
spores that land — the same empty bloom a clean tank holds.

### Light it reads

Green water lives through the whole column, so it reads the mean PAR over it —
Beer–Lambert averaged from surface to floor, a little above the substrate
figure. Film reads the light at the surfaces it coats, weighted by area: the
walls span the column and read its mean; the floor and the hardscape lie under
the canopy and read what it leaves of the substrate PAR.

### Light taken

Both kinds take light where it passes through them, on one coefficient per gram
of tissue ([Light](/subsystems/light/#the-blooms)). Green water joins the
water's extinction by its tissue per litre, so the column it reads darkens as it
thickens — a bloom self-limits — and so does every surface under it: the film
on the walls and the floor, and every leaf by the depth above it. Film coats
every lit surface at its coverage, leaves included, and a leaf passes
`1 − coverage × (1 − e^(−κσ))` of its light; the glass it coats stands beside
the lamp's path, not in it. Neither kind charges a plant anything: a shaded
plant earns less, and past its edge starves.

### Gases and nutrients

Its tissue is organic matter at the plants' recipe. A gram of it is the same
rate units a gram of leaf is, run at the kind's growth rate, so a dense bloom
strips CO₂ by day and draws oxygen down by night. Growth draws its N, P, K and
Fe from the water in the same pooled call as the plants; shed, dead and scrubbed
tissue goes back as waste, so a crashing bloom spikes ammonia as it rots.

Each kind takes its nitrogen as the plants do — ammonia first, nitrate for what
ammonia leaves — on its own affinities. A cycled tank's trace of ammonia meets
most of film's need and little of green water's; ammonia rising past that trace
— a new tank, an overfed one, a melt — feeds green water fully.

### Blackouts

Starvation reads the day's light, so a scheduled night costs nothing. A dead
fixture starves a bloom as its day empties: the bank goes in hours, green
water dies back in about a day and film in about two — the hobby's blackout,
which costs a carpet its bank and a few points of condition. Relit, both grow
again from spores that land healthy. Kept dark, the spores starve in turn, and
their dust dies back over and over.

## Key tunables and traits

| Name | Meaning | Green water | Film |
|---|---|---|---|
| `habitat` | Where it lives | column | surfaces |
| `growthRate` | Bank-to-mass conversion, healing rate, and the pace its tissue fixes, respires and starves at, on the plants' scale | `60` | `20` |
| `hardiness` | Scales every harm by `1 − hardiness` | `0.4` | `0.4` |
| `lowLight` | PAR at the bottom of its band: its daily light edge over a care-sheet day, and `Ik` at `saturationIrradianceFactor ×` it | `6` PAR | `6` PAR |
| `nutrientDemand` | Its demand tier for potassium and iron, as a plant's | low | low |
| Temperature · pH bands | Where comfort peaks and harm starts | `12–34` °C · `5.5–9.5` | `12–34` °C · `5.5–9.5` |
| `co2HalfSaturation` | CO₂ it fixes at half rate on | `0.3` mg/L | `0.3` mg/L |
| `ammoniaHalfSaturation` | Total ammonia, as NH₃, it meets half its nitrogen need on | `0.5` ppm | `0.02` ppm |
| `nitrateHalfSaturation` | Nitrate it meets half its nitrogen need on | `40` ppm | `10` ppm |
| `phosphateHalfSaturation` | Phosphate it meets half its phosphorus need on | `0.2` ppm | `0.02` ppm |
| `tissueDensity` | Organic matter a full habitat holds | `0.03` g/L | `0.0003` g/cm² |
| `sporeRate` | Mass that lands an hour, through the taper | `0.002` | `0.002` |
| `allelopathySeverity` | Harm per rate unit of thriving plant per litre, on either kind | `0.5` %/h | |
| `algaeAttenuationPerGram` | Light a gram of tissue takes per cm² crossed, on either kind: green water's full bloom adds 0.09 /cm to the water's extinction, film's full coat passes 41 % of a leaf's light | `3000` cm²/g | |

All but the last two are traits, beside the plant species; `allelopathySeverity`
and `algaeAttenuationPerGram` — with [Light](/subsystems/light/#key-tunables) —
are tunables.
