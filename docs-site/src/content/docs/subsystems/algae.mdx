## What it models

A bloom is plant mechanics without a position. It has a condition and an energy
budget — a reserve, tissue and four nutrient stores — on the
[vitality](/concepts/vitality/) model, and its light curve, income, harms,
shedding and budget are the [plants'](/subsystems/plants/), on the plants'
constants and its own traits. It runs at κ = 1, keeping no plantlet buffer:
its growth is its reproduction. It has no
height or footprint: in their place is a habitat it fills, and spores land in
it and join it.

The tank holds two kinds on that one model, run through one code path. They differ by habitat and by traits, and the tank's
conditions pick the winner — there is no split and no coded preference.

| Kind | Habitat | Size of it | Light it reads | Light taken from | Taken out by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Green water | The water column | The tank's litres | The column's mean | Everything below it — itself, the film, each plant by its depth | A water change, by the share changed |
| Film | The lit surfaces that do not grow: the glass walls, the floor and the hardscape | cm² of them | The walls at the column's mean, the floor and hardscape at what the canopy leaves, weighted by area | Every leaf, at its coverage | A scrub, off the glass; a bed swap, off the floor |

Coverage, 0–100, is the bloom's tissue over what its full habitat holds at the
kind's tissue density, so a change of habitat moves coverage, not tissue: a
piece of hardscape set down thins film's coverage and keeps its grams. Film's
habitat moves only with the hardscape, never with the planting: the leaves
wear its coverage but are not habitat.

## How it works

### What feeds it and what harms it

| Channel | Side | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| CO₂ | income | Monod on dissolved CO₂ at its `co2HalfSaturation` — far under any plant's, since algae take bicarbonate too |
| Temperature · pH | income, then harm | The plants' band helpers on its own bands: comfort peaks mid-band, damage starts at the edge |
| Starvation | harm | The share of the hour's upkeep its budget left unpaid: a bloom burns fast, so it starves fast |
| Nutrient deficiency | harm | Its Droop limit under `droopEdge` of its full stores' |

Every harm is hardened by the kind's hardiness; income, earned on its reserve
and stores, runs through the night.

### The budget

A bloom runs the [plants' budget](/subsystems/plants/#the-budget): uptake fills
its stores, photosynthesis its reserve, and the reserve pays maintenance first
and builds tissue with the rest, at the Droop limit of its emptiest store. Its
room to grow is the share of its habitat still empty, so growth slows as
coverage nears 100 and stops there. A full reserve caps what it fixes, so a
bloom on spent stores fills with carbon it cannot use.

It lives on its stores after the water runs lean, then fades: green water put
at 30 % into an unplanted tank with no nitrogen or phosphorus, the fish unfed
and a quarter of the water changed weekly, stands at 36 % after a week, 18 %
after a month and 5 % after three.

### Allelopathy

Thriving plants' exudates reach a bloom through its budget: they lower its
assimilation ceiling by hyperbolic inhibition on thriving leaf per litre,
halving it where `allelopathySeverity` times that dose is 1. A held-back bloom
fixes, draws CO₂ and earns less, and takes no damage. Plants hold green water
back twice over: they draw on the same water and cut what it fixes.

### Spores

Spores are the bloom's immigration, asked of the water each hour at
`sporeRate` through the taper and built at Q_min in the same pooled draw as
every store, so water short of a nutrient builds fewer. They land unfed —
tissue and stores, no reserve — once the bloom's losses are taken, joining a
standing bloom at its condition. Only an empty bloom, died back or never stood,
starts afresh from them at 100.

### Light

Each kind reads the mean PAR over its habitat, and takes light by Beer–Lambert
on its coverage ([Light](/subsystems/light/#the-blooms)). Green water joins the water's
extinction in proportion to its coverage, so the column it reads darkens as it
thickens — a bloom self-limits — and so does every surface under it: the film
on the walls and the floor, and every leaf by the depth above it. Film coats
every lit surface at its coverage, leaves included; the glass it coats stands
beside the lamp's path, not in it. Neither kind charges a plant anything: a
shaded plant fixes less, and starves only once its reserve can no longer pay.

### Gases and nutrients

Its tissue is organic matter at its kind's Q_min. A gram of it is the same rate
units a gram of leaf is, run at the kind's growth rate, so a dense bloom strips
CO₂ by day and draws oxygen down by night. Shed and dead tissue goes back as waste,
and a die-back's stores leach to the water, nitrogen as nitrate — so a crashing
bloom lifts nitrate at once and spikes ammonia as it rots.

Each kind takes its nitrogen as the plants do — ammonia first, nitrate for what
ammonia leaves — on its own affinities. A cycled tank's trace of ammonia and a
few ppm of nitrate meet most of film's need, so a lean tank feeds it. The same
water meets less of green water's, and ammonia rising past the trace — a new
tank, an overfed one, a melt — meets most of it. A thinly planted, brightly lit
tank can go green even when maintained, on leaner water than a well-planted one
keeps clear.

A scrub takes the walls' share of the film: its tissue and reserve into the
water as waste, and its stores leached straight to it, so KH dips by the proton
per nitrogen that nitrate's uptake took. A water change takes its share of the
green water out, stores and all.

### Blackouts

A dark tank starves a bloom on its reserve, not on the day. From 10 % coverage
green water dies back in about two weeks and film a day or two later, and the
log says so. Spores keep landing, start the bloom afresh at 100 and starve in
turn, so the dust dies back every two weeks while the dark lasts — too thin to
show, so the keeper sees clear water and a clean glass, and no more die-backs.
Relit, film is back past 20 % within a month.

## Key tunables and traits

The tunables are listed in the reference under [Algae](/reference/tunables/#algae)
and the bloom optics under [Optics](/reference/tunables/#optics). The table
holds each kind's traits, defined beside the plant species.

| Name | Meaning | Green water | Film |
|---|---|---|---|
| `habitat` | Where it lives | column | surfaces |
| `growthRate` | The pace its tissue fixes and burns at, on the plants' scale | `10` | `4` |
| `hardiness` | Scales every harm by `1 − hardiness` | `0.4` | `0.4` |
| `lowLight` | PAR at the bottom of its band: its rated day over a care-sheet day, and `Ik` at `saturationIrradianceFactor ×` it | `6` PAR | `6` PAR |
| `nutrientDemand` | Its demand tier for potassium and iron, as a plant's | low | low |
| Temperature · pH bands | Where comfort peaks and harm starts, at least as wide as every plant's | `12–34` °C · `4.5–9.5` | `12–34` °C · `4.5–9.5` |
| `co2HalfSaturation` | CO₂ it fixes at half rate on | `0.3` mg/L | `0.3` mg/L |
| `ammoniaHalfSaturation` | Total ammonia, as NH₃, it meets half its nitrogen need on | `0.5` ppm | `0.02` ppm |
| `nitrateHalfSaturation` | Nitrate it meets half its nitrogen need on | `25` ppm | `1` ppm |
| `phosphateHalfSaturation` | Phosphate it meets half its phosphorus need on | `0.2` ppm | `0.02` ppm |
| `tissueDensity` | Organic matter a full habitat holds | `0.54` g/L | `0.0045` g/cm² |
| `sporeRate` | Coverage that lands an hour, through the taper | `0.0002` | `0.002` |
| `quotaMin` | Q_min of N · P · K · Fe | `25 · 1.5 · 4 · 0.03` mg/g | `34 · 1.7 · 4 · 0.03` mg/g |
| `quotaMax` | Q_max of the same | `100 · 15 · 22 · 0.2` mg/g | `80 · 10 · 22 · 0.2` mg/g |
| `storeFillDays` | Days an empty store takes to fill from saturating water, at its opening rate | `1` | `2` |
