## What it models

The bloom is plant mechanics without a position. It has a condition, a bank
and a mass on the [vitality](/concepts/vitality/) model, and its light curve,
income, harms, shedding, bank and tissue recipe are the
[plants'](/subsystems/plants/), on the plants' constants and its own traits. It
has no height or footprint: in their place is a habitat it fills, and its
offshoots are spores that land in it and join it.

Mass is how full that habitat is, 0–100. The habitat is the water column, so a
full bloom's tissue scales with the tank's litres. Clear and green are not
coded: plants beat a bloom or lose to it on the rates.

## How it works

### What feeds it and what harms it

| Channel | Side | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| CO₂ | income | Monod on dissolved CO₂ at its `co2HalfSaturation` — far under any plant's, since algae take bicarbonate too |
| Temperature · pH | income, then harm | The plants' band helpers on its own bands: comfort peaks mid-band, damage starts at the edge |
| Light starvation | harm | The day's light over the column against its daily light edge, at respiration's Q10 and its pace: a bloom respires fast, so it starves fast |
| Nutrient deficiency | harm | Liebig sufficiency under `sufficiencyEdge`, on the light curve |
| Allelopathy | harm | `allelopathySeverity × Σ(plant rate units × condition/100) / litres` |

Every harm is hardened by the bloom's hardiness. Income runs only while the lamp
is on; the bank carries it through the night.

### The bank buys mass

The rate is the plants': `growthDrawRate` of the bank at their conversion,
`growthRate × sizePerSurplus`, is the bloom's specific growth rate `r` for the
hour. Mass follows the logistic exactly over that hour,

```
m₁ = 100·m₀ / (m₀ + (100 − m₀)·e⁻ʳ)
```

and every bank point buys the same e-fold, so the bank pays
`100·ln(m₁ / m₀) / (growthRate × sizePerSurplus)` — never more than the plants'
draw through the taper would. Water that supplies only part of the tissue is
priced the same way: the bank pays for the e-folds that arrived. A small bloom
multiplies, a full habitat buys nothing, and an empty one buys and pays nothing.
On a lit day's income a bloom doubles in two or three days.

### Spores

Spores are the bloom's offshoots. They come at `sporeRate` an hour, through the
taper at the mass the hour's growth reaches at full supply — their tissue is
drawn in the same call as that growth — and land once the bloom's own losses are
taken. They arrive at condition 100 on an empty bank and join the bloom by mass,
so a struggling bloom is lifted by their share of it and its bank is diluted by
the same share. A bloom that died back, or an empty one, is replaced by the
spores that land — the same empty bloom a clean tank holds.

### Light, gases, nutrients

The bloom lives through the whole column, so it reads the mean PAR over it —
Beer–Lambert averaged from surface to floor, a little above the substrate
figure. It saturates at low light.

Its tissue is organic matter at the plants' recipe. A gram of it is the same
rate units a gram of leaf is, run at the bloom's pace, so a dense bloom
strips CO₂ by day and draws oxygen down by night. Growth draws its N, P, K and
Fe from the water in the same pooled call as the plants; shed, dead and scrubbed
tissue goes back as waste, so a crashing bloom spikes ammonia as it rots.

### Blackouts

Starvation reads the day's light, so a scheduled night costs nothing. A dead
fixture starves the bloom as its day empties: the bank goes in hours and the
condition within two days, and the bloom dies back — the hobby's blackout, which
costs a plant its bank and little else. Relit, it grows again from spores that
land healthy. Kept dark, the spores starve in turn, and their dust dies back
about every day and a half.

## Key tunables and traits

| Name | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| `growthRate` | Bank-to-mass conversion and healing rate, on the plants' scale | `30` |
| `pace` | How many times faster than a leaf its tissue fixes, respires and starves: its growth rate | `30` |
| `hardiness` | Scales every harm by `1 − hardiness` | `0.4` |
| `lowLight` | PAR at the bottom of its band: its daily light edge over a care-sheet day, and `Ik` at `saturationIrradianceFactor ×` it | `6` PAR |
| `nutrientDemand` | Its demand tier, as a plant's | low |
| Temperature · pH bands | Where comfort peaks and harm starts | `12–34` °C · `5.5–9.5` |
| `co2HalfSaturation` | CO₂ it fixes at half rate on | `0.3` mg/L |
| `tissuePerLitre` | Organic matter in a litre of full bloom | `0.03` g |
| `sporeRate` | Mass that lands an hour, through the taper | `0.002` |
| `allelopathySeverity` | Harm per rate unit of thriving plant per litre | `3` %/h |

All but the last are traits, beside the plant species; `allelopathySeverity` is a tunable.
